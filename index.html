OUR STORY

Great adventures don't have to take you far.

Ireland has a way of surprising you. From windswept coastlines and lively cities to quiet countryside hideaways, there's always something new around the corner.

Emerald Escapes brings together thoughtfully selected experiences for curious travellers, couples, families and anyone looking to make the most of their time away.

Emerald Escapes began with three friends from Galway, Cork and Dublin who shared a curiosity for the places closer to home. What started as spontaneous weekend road trips quickly became a collection of favourite destinations, hidden gems and carefully planned adventures.

Today, Emerald Escapes brings together thoughtfully selected experiences across Ireland, from coastal adventures and city breaks to peaceful countryside retreats. Whether you're travelling solo, planning a family holiday or escaping for a weekend with someone special, we believe there's always something new waiting to be discovered.
